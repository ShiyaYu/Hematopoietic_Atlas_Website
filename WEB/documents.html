Overview

Data browser

Data browser incorporates two cellxgene plug-ins, offering interactive visualization interfaces for integrated data on both hematopoietic stem and progenitor cell atlas and mature hematopoietic cell atlas.

Portraits of hematopoietic Atlas

Portraits of tissues provides the description of the tissue functioning in hematopoiesis in tissue scale. We calculate the highly expressed genes across tissues and visualize results in molecular scale.

Portraits of cell types provides the proportion distribution across the tissues in tissue scale and calculate the highly expressed genes of the main cell types across tissues in molecular scale. The results of cell-cell interaction are shown in the corresponding scale.

Portrait of genes provides gene introduction, the expression distribution of the selected gene across tissues and cell types in the corresponding scale.

User Guides

This guide will help you understand how to navigate the Data Browser and get results of portraits effectively using the dynamic tools provided.

1. Browsing the Data

Go to the Data Browser in the top navbar and choose a subset (e.g., HSPC or Blood Atlas). The interactive interface allows you to filter cell clusters based on metadata parameters like tissue origin, sequencing technology, or cluster identities.

2. Exploring Portraits

Under the Portraits section, detailed information of tissues, genes and cell types of hematopoietic stem and progenitor cell atlas and mature hematopoietic cell atlas can be found in the corresponding portraits:

  • Portrait of tissues provides tissue description, cell type proportion, highly expressed genes and enriched GO terms in each tissue. Users could find instructions in the video below:
  • Portrait of cell types provides proportion, highly expressed genes and enriched GO terms across tissues in each cell type. And cell-cell interactions among cell types are also presented. Users could find instructions in the video below:
  • Portrait of genes provides the expression distribution of the selected gene across cell types and tissues. The ridge plots show the non-zero expression distributions in different cell types. The number at the right of the ridge plots show the non-zero percentages of the expression values. Users could find instructions in the video below:

3. Downloading Data

Raw data accesses and original papers can be freely obtained directly from the Download page for exploring.
